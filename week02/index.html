I'm Alyssa and I am a sophomore Digital Media & Design student concentrating in Web / Interactive Media Design.

I would still possibly want to pursue making movies and television shows, but web design is still one of my biggest passions.

My favorite websites are the ones that help me with my design projects and creative writing. I use them to find inspiration, learn new skills, and stay organized. I only began coding regularly last fall, as I had recently switched my concentration to web design. I first discovered my passion for it during our Foundations classes.
